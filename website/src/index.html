A library that moves with you.

Target experience

Designed for Android, iPhone, iPad and the web.

The goal is to browse and synchronise your library without personally maintaining a server that must stay online. Live library access and device synchronisation are being developed.

Recovery still needs your credentials and enough available storage participants.

Personal moments. Personal boundaries.

Private photos, faces and locations need explicit boundaries.

{{key_icon}}

Your recovery keys

Keep an independent private copy of the key and credentials needed for recovery. Storage participants do not receive your decryption key.

{{lock_icon}}

Encrypted storage

The snapshot is encrypted before it reaches storage participants, including the media and library metadata inside it.

{{shield_icon}}

Careful processing

Private image analysis requires local or explicitly trusted execution. Encrypted storage alone does not make arbitrary remote processing private.

Private media and metadata are not automatically published to public cache or used for model training.

What works today. What comes next.

{{check_icon}}

Demonstrated in a scoped trial

An encrypted snapshot made from synthetic test data was stored across three providers. With one provider offline, the remaining providers supplied the encrypted data for two successful local decryptions and restores by the owner.

The recorded trial also completed deletion of its stored copies and cleanup.
